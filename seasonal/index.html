Where to buy it local

Farmers markets, CSAs, on-farm markets, and food hubs from the USDA Local Food Directory, within of .

Seasonality data is compiled by region from the ACL/Farmers' Almanac regional seasonal produce chart, the Seasonal Food Guide (NRDC and state agriculture departments), and state university extension harvest calendars. Harvest windows shift with weather and microclimate — treat these as a guide, and your local market as the final word.

Market listings come from the USDA Local Food Directory, including the coordinates plotted on the map. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap contributors. Storage guidance and ethylene ratings are the same data behind the SimplyKrisp Produce Storage Guide.
