Keep the produce you buy fresh long enough to eat it.

Produce usually gets tossed for three reasons: it spoils faster than expected, it gets buried in the fridge, or there’s more than you can use. Each SimplyKrisp tool handles one part of that.

1Store it rightThe Storage Guide and Crisper Map show where each item lasts longest.2Buy what’s in seasonFresher at the store means more days at home.3Slow the spoilingA Krisp in each crisper drawer pulls out the ethylene that ages greens and broccoli.4Eat it in timeMy Krisper (beta) reads your grocery receipts and tells you what to use first.5See the proofEvery home test, published in the Lab Notebook, including the ones that show no difference.

Ethylene thresholds cited in test questions come from the UC Davis Postharvest Technology Center produce facts sheets for broccoli and romaine. Home tests show what happened in one refrigerator under the conditions noted; they are not controlled laboratory studies and do not establish universal shelf-life gains.
