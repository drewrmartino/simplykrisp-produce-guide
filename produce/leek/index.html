How to store leeks, and why they go bad

Leeks keep a week or two. They soften around ethylene, the tops wilt as they dry out, and damaged layers turn slimy.

Where to keep them
Fridge
How long they last
1-2 weeks (refrigerated)
Best temperature
About 32°F (0°C)
Don't store next to
Apples and pears
What are you seeing?
Soft outer layersWilted, floppy topsSlimy layers

How to store leeks

  • Store unwashed and untrimmed in a loose plastic bag in the crisper drawer
  • Keep the bag closed. Leeks pass their smell to other food
  • Keep them away from apples, pears and other ethylene producers
  • Slice them lengthwise and rinse between the layers just before cooking

Picking good leeks: Firm, straight white stalks with crisp, dark green tops. Skip any with yellow or slimy leaves.

Why leeks go bad

Soft outer layers

Ethylene gasTrim and use

What you'll seeThe stalk loses its snap and the outer layers go soft.

Why it happens

USDA's storage handbook rates leeks as moderately sensitive to ethylene and lists softening and more decay as the effects. 1

Is it still OK?

Safe to eat. Peel off the soft outer layer and use the firm stalk underneath soon.

Prevent it next time
  • Keep leeks away from apples and pears
  • Keep them cold, in a bag
Use it up
  • Cook them into soup
  • Braise or roast them
Where a Krisp fits

A Krisp helps a little here. Slows the softening ethylene causes. Expect about 1–2 extra days in a closed crisper drawer. See the Double Krisp

Wilted, floppy tops

Drying outStill good

What you'll seeThe green tops droop and dry out at the tips.

Why it happens

USDA's storage handbook says high humidity is essential to keep leeks from wilting. Open fridge air is much drier than they need. 1

Is it still OK?

Still good. Trim the dry tips. The white and pale green part is what most recipes use anyway.

Prevent it next time
  • Keep them in a bag in the crisper drawer
  • Leave the tops on until you cook them
Use it up
  • Save the dark tops for stock

Slimy layers

Bacterial rotToss it

What you'll seeOuter layers turn dark, wet and slimy, and smell bad.

Why it happens

Leeks get the same soft rots as onions. They start where the stalk was bruised or stayed wet. 12

Is it still OK?

Toss a leek that is slimy or smells bad. If only the outermost layer is affected, peel it away and rinse the firm stalk well.

Prevent it next time
  • Store leeks unwashed
  • Do not crush them under heavier produce

What went wrong?

Leeks and ethylene

Ethylene is a gas that ripening produce gives off. In a closed crisper drawer it builds up and makes sensitive produce yellow, soften or sprout sooner.

  • Leeks give off almost no ethylene, so they are safe to store next to most other produce.
  • Leeks are somewhat sensitive to ethylene, so storing them away from ripening fruit helps.

Keep them apart from apples and pears.

The part people throw away

About 56% of leeks by weight is usually trimmed off as dark green tops and roots (USDA FoodData Central). Some of it can still be used:

  • Simmer the dark tops in stock. They are too tough to eat but full of flavor
  • Slice the tops very thin and braise them until soft
