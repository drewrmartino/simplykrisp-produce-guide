How to store grapes, and why they go bad

Grapes don't ripen after harvest and barely react to ethylene. They last one to three weeks cold, and usually end by drying out, dropping off the stem or growing gray mold.

Where to keep them
Fridge
How long they last
1-3 weeks (refrigerated)
Best temperature
About 32°F (0°C)
Don't store next to
Nothing in particular
What are you seeing?
Brown, slipping skins and gray fuzzGrapes falling off the stemWrinkled grapes and dry stems

How to store grapes

  • Store unwashed in their original ventilated bag or container
  • Keep in the back of the fridge where it's coldest
  • Do not seal in an airtight container
  • Wash only before eating

Picking good grapes: Plump, firmly attached to the stem, with a powdery bloom on the skin.

Does a Krisp help? Tested directly: continuous ethylene had no effect on table grapes in cold storage.

Why grapes go bad

Brown, slipping skins and gray fuzz

MoldToss it

What you'll seeGrapes turn brown, the skin slips off easily, and white threads or gray fuzz appear, usually in one part of the bunch.

Why it happens

This is gray mold, the main disease of stored grapes. It moves through a bunch quickly once it starts. 12

Is it still OK?

Toss moldy grapes and the ones touching them. You can't cut mold out of soft fruit.

Prevent it next time
  • Keep grapes cold and dry in their vented bag
  • Don't wash them until you eat them
  • Snip off damaged or moldy clusters as soon as you see them

Grapes falling off the stem

AgeStill good

What you'll seeLoose grapes collect at the bottom of the bag.

Why it happens

This is called shatter. It increases as grapes mature and with rough handling. 1

Is it still OK?

Still good if the loose grapes are firm. Rinse them and eat them first.

Prevent it next time
  • Handle the bag gently
  • Eat grapes within one to three weeks
Use it up
  • Freeze loose grapes as a snack
  • Roast them with a little oil and salt for toast or salads

Wrinkled grapes and dry stems

Drying outStill good

What you'll seeThe stems turn brown and brittle and the grapes start to wrinkle.

Why it happens

Grapes dry out in fridge air. UC Davis lists shriveled and dried berries as defects and recommends 90 to 95 percent humidity. 1

Is it still OK?

Still good if there's no mold. They taste sweeter and chewier.

Prevent it next time
  • Keep them in their bag at the back of the fridge
  • Leave grapes on the stem until you eat them
Use it up
  • Roast them or simmer them into a pan sauce

Grapes and ethylene

Ethylene is a gas that ripening produce gives off. In a closed crisper drawer it builds up and makes sensitive produce yellow, soften or sprout sooner.

  • Grapes give off almost no ethylene, so they are safe to store next to most other produce.
  • Grapes are barely affected by ethylene.

The part people throw away

About 4% of grapes by weight is usually trimmed off as stems and soft grapes (USDA FoodData Central). Some of it can still be used:

  • Freeze soft grapes as a snack or smoothie ice
  • Roast soft grapes with a little oil and salt for toast or salads
