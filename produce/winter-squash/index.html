How to store winter squash

Winter squash keeps best in a cool, dark pantry, at about 54°F, and lasts 1-3 months. Here is how to store it, what to keep it away from, and how to pick good winter squash.

Where to keep it
Cool, dark pantry
How long it lasts
1-3 months (pantry)
Best temperature
About 54°F (12°C)
Don't store next to
Nothing in particular

How to store winter squash

  • Store in a cool, dark, dry place with good ventilation
  • Do not refrigerate whole squash
  • Once cut, wrap and refrigerate for up to 5 days
  • Keep each squash from touching to prevent rot from spreading

Picking good winter squash: Hard skin that can't be dented with a fingernail, heavy for its size, with a dry, corky stem.

Does a Krisp help? Winter squash keeps for months in a cool pantry, well away from the fridge.

Winter squash and ethylene

Ethylene is a gas that ripening produce gives off. In a closed crisper drawer it builds up and makes sensitive produce yellow, soften or sprout sooner.

  • Winter squash gives off only a little ethylene, so it is fine next to most other produce.
  • Winter squash is somewhat sensitive to ethylene, so storing it away from ripening fruit helps.

The part people throw away

About 29% of winter squash by weight is usually trimmed off as seeds and rind (USDA FoodData Central). Some of it can still be used:

  • Rinse, dry and roast the seeds with oil and salt
  • Simmer strings and peels into stock

  • Onion
