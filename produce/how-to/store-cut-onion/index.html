How to store a cut onion

Short answer

Put the cut onion in a sealed container or wrap it tightly and refrigerate it. Use it within about 7 days. Cut onions aren't poisonous. That's an old internet myth. If you won't use it in time, dice it and freeze it for cooking.

How to store a cut or half onion

  1. Refrigerate it within two hours of cutting. The FDA advises refrigerating all cut produce within that window.1
  2. Put it in a sealed container or wrap it tightly. This keeps the smell in and stops the cut face from drying out.2
  3. Use it within about 7 days.2
  4. Before using it, check for sliminess, mold or a sour smell, and toss it if you find any.3

Are cut onions poisonous?

No. A widely shared claim says leftover onions attract bacteria and become poisonous. According to the National Onion Association, it goes back to a 2008 blog post. Onion juice is actually antimicrobial, and a cut onion stored properly in the fridge is fine for up to a week.2

How to freeze onions

Dice the onion into about ¼-inch pieces. It doesn't need blanching. Pack it into freezer bags or containers with the air pressed out, or freeze the pieces on a tray first so they don't clump. It's best used within a few months, in cooked dishes. Frozen onion isn't crisp.4

Whole onions don't belong in the fridge

Uncut onions keep for a month or more in a cool, dry, dark spot.5 Only cut ones go in the fridge. See how to store onions.

Common questions

How long does a cut onion last in the fridge?

Up to about 7 days in a sealed container.2

Can I leave a cut onion on the counter?

Not for long. The FDA advises refrigerating cut produce within two hours.1

Are leftover cut onions poisonous?

No. It's a myth from a 2008 blog post. Stored sealed in the fridge, a cut onion is fine for up to a week.2

Can you freeze chopped onions?

Yes, without blanching. Use them within a few months in cooked dishes.4
