How to organize your fridge so produce lasts longer

Short answer

Keep the fridge at 40°F or below. Put vegetables that wilt in the high-humidity crisper drawer (vent closed) and fruit that rots in the low-humidity drawer (vent open). Keep apples and pears away from greens and broccoli, give your eat-first produce a spot at eye level, and leave out what belongs on the counter or in the pantry.

Start with the temperature

Set the fridge to 40°F or below, and check it with an appliance thermometer if it doesn't have one built in.1 Colder slows spoilage, but don't go below freezing, because frozen spots ruin lettuce and other tender produce.

What goes in each crisper drawer

Vegetables generally keep best in higher humidity and fruit in lower humidity. Some crisper drawers have a slider that controls this.1

High humidity: vent closed

For things that wilt.

  • Lettuce
  • Spinach
  • Kale
  • Broccoli
  • Cauliflower
  • Cabbage
  • Cucumber
  • Carrot
  • Celery
  • Green Beans
  • Asparagus
  • Bell Pepper
  • Fresh herbs

Low humidity: vent open

For things that rot.

  • Apple
  • Pear
  • Grape
  • Peach
  • Plum
  • Nectarine
  • Kiwi
  • Avocado

Stone fruit, kiwi and avocados go in the fridge only once they are ripe. Want it on paper? Print the crisper map.

Keep ethylene producers away from greens

Apples, pears and other ripening fruit give off ethylene. In a closed drawer it builds up and yellows broccoli, spots lettuce and softens cucumbers sooner.2 Keep the producers in their own drawer, and see fridge or counter for what shouldn't be in the fridge at all.

Three habits that matter more than any gadget

  • Make an eat-first spot: one place at eye level for whatever is closest to turning.
  • Check the drawers before you shop. Thirty seconds and a phone photo stop you from buying a second bag of spinach.
  • Don't overpack the drawers. Crushed produce bruises, and bruised spots spoil first.

Common questions

What goes in the high-humidity crisper drawer?

Vegetables that wilt: leafy greens, broccoli, cucumbers, carrots and herbs. Vegetables do best in higher humidity.1

What goes in the low-humidity drawer?

Fruit, which does best in lower humidity: apples, pears, grapes and ripe stone fruit.1

What temperature should my fridge be?

40°F or below.1

Why do my vegetables go bad so fast in the crisper?

Often it's ethylene from fruit sharing the drawer, trapped moisture or the drawer being too warm. Separate fruit from greens, dry produce before storing, and check the temperature.21
