How to store a cut avocado

Short answer

Squeeze lemon or lime juice over the cut flesh, then wrap it tightly with no air trapped against it, or put it cut side down in an airtight container. Refrigerate it and eat it soon. If the top browns a little, scrape it off. The flesh underneath is fine.

How to store half an avocado

  1. Squeeze lemon or lime juice over the cut flesh.12
  2. Wrap it tightly in plastic or reusable wrap so no air is trapped against the flesh, or put it cut side down in an airtight container.12
  3. Refrigerate it.1
  4. Eat it soon. A day or two is about as long as the cut surface stays looking good.

Why cut avocado turns brown

Browning is oxidation: the cut flesh reacts with air.1 Juice and a tight wrap slow it down. A thin brown layer on top isn't spoilage. Scrape it off and the green underneath is fine. Toss it if it smells sour or rancid or shows mold. Avocados are soft fruit, so you can't cut mold away.3

If it isn't ripe yet

If you cut an avocado and it's still hard, put the two halves back together, seal them in wrap, and refrigerate.1

Guacamole and mashed avocado

Put it in an airtight container and press plastic or reusable wrap right onto the surface before you close the lid.2

Don't store it in water

Submerging cut avocado in water is a popular hack, but the FDA found Listeria on about 18% of avocado skins it sampled, and FDA researchers showed it can move into the flesh in water.45

To keep it longer, freeze it as purée

Avocados don't freeze well whole or sliced. Purée them with about 1 tablespoon of lemon juice per 2 avocados, pack the purée into a container, and freeze. It's good in dips, dressings and smoothies.6

Common questions

Does leaving the pit in keep avocado from browning?

Juice and a tight wrap are what slow browning. The California Avocado Commission's leftover-avocado method removes the pit, adds lemon juice and stores the half face down in an airtight container.2

Is brown cut avocado safe to eat?

A brown surface is oxidation, not spoilage. Scrape it off. If it smells sour or rancid or is moldy, toss it.13

Can you store cut avocado in water?

It's not recommended. FDA research found Listeria can move from the skin into the flesh in water.5

Can you freeze avocado?

Yes, as purée with lemon juice. Whole or sliced avocado doesn't freeze well.6
