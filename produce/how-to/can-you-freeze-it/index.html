Can you freeze it? A freezing chart for fruits and vegetables

Short answer

Most vegetables freeze well if you blanch them first, and most fruit freezes well raw. A few don't freeze well for eating raw: lettuce, cucumbers, radishes, and raw celery and cabbage. Frozen produce kept at 0°F stays safe indefinitely and keeps its best quality for about 8 to 12 months.

How to freeze vegetables: blanch first

Blanching means a short dip in boiling water or steam. It slows the enzymes that otherwise keep breaking down flavor, color and texture in the freezer.11

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, and set a bowl of ice water next to it.
  2. Blanch the vegetable for the time in the chart.11
  3. Move it straight into the ice water to stop the cooking, then drain it well.
  4. Pack it into freezer bags or containers, press out the air, and label it with the date.
  5. Freeze it in a single layer so it freezes fast, then stack it once it's solid.23

How to freeze fruit: the tray method

Spread prepared fruit in one layer on a tray, freeze it, then pack it into containers right away. The pieces stay loose, so you can pour out just what you need.18

How long frozen produce lasts

Food kept at 0°F stays safe indefinitely. Time affects quality, not safety.23 For best quality, use frozen fruits and vegetables within about 8 to 12 months.24 Freezer burn makes dry, grayish patches. It isn't unsafe, but it tastes worse, so cut it off.23

Common questions

Can you freeze lettuce?

Not well. It turns limp and waterlogged when it thaws.233

Can you freeze mushrooms?

Yes, after steaming or sautéing them first. Steamed mushrooms keep longer than sautéed ones.1

Do you have to blanch vegetables before freezing?

It's recommended for most vegetables. Blanching stops the enzymes that cause flavor, color and texture loss in the freezer. Diced onions and raw pepper strips are exceptions.11913

Can you refreeze thawed vegetables?

If they thawed in the fridge, yes, though the quality drops. Don't refreeze food left out of the fridge for more than 2 hours.23
