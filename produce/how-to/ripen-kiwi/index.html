How to ripen kiwi, and keep ripe kiwi from going mushy

Short answer

Leave firm kiwi on the counter for 2 to 3 days, or put it in a bag with an apple or banana to speed it up. It's ripe when it gives to gentle pressure. Then move it to the fridge, where it keeps about 7 to 10 more days, but not next to apples, or it'll turn mushy.

How to ripen kiwi

  1. Leave firm kiwi on the counter at room temperature, out of direct sun. It usually ripens in 2 to 3 days.1
  2. To speed it up, put it in a paper or plastic bag with a ripe apple or banana. Their ethylene speeds up kiwi ripening.1
  3. Check it daily. A ripe kiwi yields gently to light pressure.1
  4. Once ripe, eat it or refrigerate it.

Why kiwi goes mushy in the fridge

Kiwi is extremely sensitive to ethylene. UC Davis reports that as little as 5 to 10 parts per billion makes it soften.2 That's why one apple ripens a bag of kiwi so well. It's also why ripe kiwi stored in a drawer with apples or pears turns soft and mushy within days.

How to keep ripe kiwi longer

  • Refrigerate it once it's ripe. It keeps about 7 to 10 more days.1
  • Keep it away from apples, pears and other ethylene producers.12
  • Firm kiwi in the fridge still ripens, just slowly, over about 5 to 7 days. That's handy if you want to stagger a big bag.1

Ethylene and a Krisp: Ethylene speeds kiwi softening, even in the cold. A Krisp absorbs ethylene in a closed crisper drawer, which matters most when apples, pears or bananas share the fridge. Source: USDA Agriculture Handbook 66 (2016), Kiwifruit See the Double Krisp

Common questions

How do you know when a kiwi is ripe?

It yields gently to light pressure, like a ripe peach.1

Does putting kiwi with apples make it ripen faster?

Yes. Kiwi is extremely sensitive to ethylene, and apples give off a lot of it. It's great for ripening, but keep ripe kiwi away from apples.21

Can you eat unripe kiwi?

You can, but firm green kiwi is sour. Gold kiwi is usually ready to eat when you buy it.1

How long does ripe kiwi last in the fridge?

About 7 to 10 days, as long as it's kept away from ethylene-producing fruit.1
