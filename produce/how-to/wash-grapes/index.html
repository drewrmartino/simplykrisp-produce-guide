How to wash grapes

Short answer

Rinse grapes under cool running water right before you eat them, gently rubbing them as you go, then pat them dry. The white, powdery film is bloom, a natural coating the grape makes. Keep grapes unwashed in the fridge until then.

How to wash grapes

  1. Leave them unwashed in the fridge until you're ready to eat them.1
  2. Put them in a colander and rinse with cool running water, gently rubbing them with your hands.23
  3. Pat them dry with a clean towel. Drying removes more bacteria and keeps any leftovers from getting soggy.2
  4. Pull off and toss any soft, split or moldy grapes before serving.

What is the white film on grapes?

It's called bloom. It's a natural coating that helps protect grapes from losing moisture and from decay, and it's a good sign.1 A rinse removes most of it. It isn't pesticide, and you don't need to scrub it off.

Skip the soap, baking soda and produce wash

The FDA says gently rubbing produce under plain running water is enough. It recommends against soap, detergent and commercial produce washes.24 Soaks are popular online, but you don't need them to clean grapes.

Why you shouldn't wash grapes before storing them

Gray mold is the main disease of stored grapes, and it moves through a bunch quickly once it starts.5 Grapes washed and put away damp give it a head start. Store them dry in a ventilated bag in the coldest part of the fridge, where they keep for one to three weeks. See how to store grapes.

Common questions

Is the white stuff on grapes pesticide?

No. It's bloom, a natural coating that helps protect grapes from moisture loss and decay. Rinsing removes most of it.1

Do you need to soak grapes in vinegar or baking soda?

No. The FDA says rubbing produce under plain running water is enough, and it recommends against produce washes.2

Should you wash grapes before refrigerating them?

No. Keep them unwashed in the fridge and rinse them just before eating. Moisture helps gray mold spread.15

Can you freeze grapes?

Yes. Stem, wash and dry seedless grapes, then freeze them whole. They're good as a frozen snack or in smoothies.67
