How to tell if a mango is ripe, and how to ripen one

Short answer

Squeeze it gently. A ripe mango gives slightly, like a ripe peach or avocado, and often smells fruity at the stem. Don't go by color, because red skin doesn't mean ripe. A firm mango will ripen on the counter in a few days.

Squeeze it, don't judge by color

Mangoes come in many colors, and the red blush on some varieties isn't a sign of ripeness.12 Go by feel instead:

  • Ripe: it gives slightly when you squeeze gently.1
  • Medium-ripe: it's somewhat firm.1
  • Unripe: it's very firm to the touch.1
  • Smell the stem end. Ripe mangoes often have a fruity aroma there.1

How to ripen a hard mango

  1. Leave it on the counter at room temperature. UC Davis ripens mangoes at 68 to 72°F, which is about room temperature.2
  2. To speed it up, put it in a paper bag. Ethylene speeds up mango ripening, and a bag holds in the gas the fruit gives off.2
  3. Check it daily with a gentle squeeze. Depending on how mature it was when picked, a mango can take anywhere from a few days to more than a week.2

Once it's ripe

Move a ripe mango to the fridge to hold it a few more days. Don't refrigerate firm, unripe mangoes. They're tropical fruit, and UC Davis stores even ripe mangoes at about 50°F, warmer than a home fridge, to avoid chilling damage.2 For cut mango, use an airtight container in the fridge.

Does a Krisp help? Mangoes ripen on the counter, and once ripe they have only a day or two, so they rarely share a drawer with a Krisp.

Common questions

Is a red mango ripe?

Not necessarily. The red on some varieties isn't a ripeness indicator. Squeeze gently instead, because a ripe mango gives slightly.12

Can you eat an unripe mango?

Yes. Mangoes can be eaten at any stage. Unripe ones taste sour and tart, and ripe ones taste sweet.1

How long does it take a mango to ripen?

Usually several days to a week on the counter, depending on how mature it was when picked. A paper bag speeds it up.2

Should mangoes be refrigerated?

Only once they're ripe. Firm mangoes are tropical fruit and ripen best at room temperature.2
