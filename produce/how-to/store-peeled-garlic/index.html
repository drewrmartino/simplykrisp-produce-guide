How to store peeled garlic

Short answer

Put peeled garlic cloves in a covered container in the fridge and use them within about a week. To keep them longer, freeze them. If you cover garlic with oil, refrigerate it and use it within 4 days, or freeze it, because garlic in oil at room temperature can cause botulism.

How to store peeled garlic cloves

  1. Put the cloves in a covered container.1
  2. Refrigerate them.1
  3. Use them within about a week.1
  4. Toss any that turn soft, slimy or moldy.2

Garlic in oil: refrigerate it and use it within 4 days

Homemade garlic in oil is a botulism risk. Garlic can carry Clostridium botulinum spores, and covering it in oil cuts off oxygen. Unrefrigerated, that lets the bacteria make a toxin you can't taste or smell.3

Make garlic in oil fresh, keep it at 40°F or below, and use it within 4 days, or freeze it for longer storage.45 Throw it out if it's been at room temperature for more than two hours.3

How to freeze garlic

Whole bulbs and unpeeled cloves can go in a freezer bag with the air pressed out.1 Garlic in oil can also be frozen, packed in freezer jars or containers, for up to several months.4

Whole bulbs belong in the pantry

A whole bulb keeps for months in a cool, dry, airy spot. The fridge is close to the temperature where garlic sprouts fastest.6 Once you break a bulb apart, the cloves keep only a few days to about 10 days.31 See how to store garlic.

Common questions

How long does peeled garlic last in the fridge?

About a week in a covered container.1

Is it safe to store garlic in olive oil?

Only in the fridge for up to 4 days, or in the freezer. Garlic in oil at room temperature is a botulism risk.43

Can you freeze peeled garlic?

Yes. Freeze whole or unpeeled cloves in a sealed freezer bag, or freeze garlic in oil in freezer containers.14

Should whole garlic be refrigerated?

No. Keep whole bulbs in a cool, dry pantry. Fridge temperatures encourage sprouting.6
