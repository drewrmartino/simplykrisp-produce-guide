How to store pomegranate seeds

Short answer

Refrigerate pomegranate seeds in an airtight container and eat them within a few days, no more than about a week. To keep them longer, freeze them in a single layer on a tray, then bag them. A whole pomegranate keeps for a month or more in the fridge, so leave the seeds in the fruit until you need them.

How to store the seeds

  1. Pat the seeds (arils) dry and pick out any bits of white membrane.
  2. Put them in an airtight container and refrigerate.1
  3. Eat them within a few days. Guidance runs from a few days to about a week, so sooner is better.21
  4. Store-bought cups of seeds come chilled. Keep them in the fridge and go by the date on the package.1

How to freeze pomegranate seeds

Spread the seeds in one layer on a tray and freeze them, then move them to an airtight container or freezer bag. Take out only what you need. The University of Arkansas Extension says they keep for up to 6 months frozen.23 Thawed seeds are softer, so they're better in smoothies, sauces or baking than as a garnish.

How to get the seeds out

Cut the pomegranate in half across the middle. Hold one half cut side down over a bowl and rap the shell firmly with the back of a wooden spoon so the seeds fall out.4 Pull out any white pith that falls in with them.

Leave them in the fruit if you can

Whole pomegranates keep for one to three months in the fridge, much longer than loose seeds. They mostly fail by drying out, but rot inside can hide behind normal-looking skin.5 Toss seeds that are brown, mushy, smell fermented or show mold. See why pomegranates go bad.

Common questions

How long do pomegranate seeds last in the fridge?

A few days to about a week in an airtight container. Eat them sooner rather than later.21

Can you freeze pomegranate seeds?

Yes. Freeze them on a tray in a single layer, then pack them airtight. The University of Arkansas Extension says they keep for up to 6 months.2

How long does a whole pomegranate last?

One to three months in the fridge. It mostly fails by drying out.51

How do you know if pomegranate seeds have gone bad?

Brown or black seeds, a mushy texture, a fermented smell or any mold means toss them.56
