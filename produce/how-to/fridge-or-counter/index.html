Fridge or counter? Where every fruit and vegetable goes

Short answer

Most vegetables, berries, apples, grapes and citrus go in the fridge. Avocados, pears, kiwi, mangoes, stone fruit and melons ripen on the counter, then go in the fridge once ripe. Bananas, tomatoes and basil stay on the counter. Potatoes, onions, garlic, sweet potatoes and winter squash go in a cool, dark pantry.

Why some produce stays out of the fridge

  • Tropical and warm-season produce is damaged by cold. Bananas are hurt below about 56°F,1 and tomatoes lose flavor and ripen unevenly after long spells below 50°F.2
  • Some fruit needs to ripen first. Avocados and pears ripen at room temperature, and cold before ripening can stop them from ripening properly.34
  • Potatoes, onions and garlic are dry-storage crops. They do best in a cool, dark, airy spot. Garlic sprouts fastest at about 40 to 65°F, which is close to fridge temperature.56

Common questions

Should apples be refrigerated?

Yes. Apples keep for weeks in the fridge and soften much faster on the counter. UC Davis stores them close to 32°F. Keep them away from greens, because apples give off a lot of ethylene.7

Should tomatoes be refrigerated?

Keep them on the counter until ripe. Long spells below 50°F cost flavor and texture. If a ripe tomato won't be eaten in a day or two, a few days in the fridge is OK.2

Should avocados be refrigerated?

Only once ripe. Cold stops unripe avocados from ripening properly, but it slows down ripe ones.3

Should bananas be refrigerated?

Not until they're ripe. Below about 56°F the peel discolors and green bananas may stop ripening. Once ripe, the fridge buys a few days.1

Should potatoes be refrigerated?

No. Keep them in a cool, dark, ventilated spot. Light turns them green, and warmth makes them sprout sooner.89

Should onions be refrigerated?

Whole onions go in a cool, dry, dark spot. Once cut, they go in the fridge in a sealed container.610

Do oranges need to be refrigerated?

They keep longest in the fridge, often for weeks.11

Does garlic need to be refrigerated?

Not whole bulbs. They keep for months in a cool, dry pantry. Peeled cloves go in the fridge.1213
