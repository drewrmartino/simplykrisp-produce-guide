Produce how-to guides

Short, sourced answers to the questions people actually search for: how to ripen it, how to wash it, how to store what's left, and whether it goes in the fridge, on the counter or in the freezer.

Ripening

  • How to ripen avocados fast, and how to slow them downPut a hard avocado in a closed bag with an apple or banana to ripen it faster. How to tell when it's ripe, when to refrigerate, and the water hack to skip.
  • How to tell if a mango is ripe, and how to ripen oneSqueeze, don't look: color is not a reliable sign of a ripe mango. How to check ripeness, ripen a hard mango on the counter, and when to refrigerate it.
  • How to ripen bananas faster, or keep them from ripeningBag green bananas with an apple to ripen them faster. To slow ripe bananas, refrigerate them: the peel darkens but the fruit stays good for a few more days.
  • How to ripen kiwi, and keep ripe kiwi from going mushyRipen firm kiwi on the counter in 2 to 3 days, or faster in a bag with an apple or banana. Then refrigerate it, away from apples, so it doesn't go mushy.
  • How to ripen pears, and how to tell when they're readyPears ripen on the counter, not the tree. Speed them up in a paper bag with an apple or banana, check the neck daily, then refrigerate ripe pears.

Washing

  • How to wash strawberries and other berries, and whether vinegar helpsWash strawberries right before eating, under cool running water, then pat them dry. What the FDA says about soap and produce washes, and what a vinegar soak does.
  • How to wash grapesRinse grapes under cool running water right before eating and pat them dry. The white powdery film is bloom, a natural coating, not pesticide. No soap needed.
  • How to wash vegetables and lettuceRinse vegetables under cool running water, scrub firm ones with a brush, and soak leafy greens. When to skip washing, and why soap and produce washes aren't needed.

Storing leftovers

  • How to store a cut onionStore a cut or half onion in a sealed container in the fridge and use it within about 7 days. Cut onions aren't poisonous. How to freeze chopped onion.
  • How to store pomegranate seedsStore pomegranate seeds in an airtight container in the fridge and eat them within a few days to a week, or freeze them on a tray. How to get the seeds out cleanly.
  • How to store a cut avocadoSqueeze lemon or lime juice on cut avocado, wrap it tightly with no air, and refrigerate. Why it browns, how to store guacamole, and why not to store it in water.
  • How to store peeled garlicRefrigerate peeled garlic cloves in a covered container and use them within about a week, or freeze them. Why homemade garlic in oil needs special care.

Fridge, counter and freezer

  • Fridge or counter? Where every fruit and vegetable goesWhich fruits and vegetables go in the fridge, which stay on the counter, which ripen first, and which belong in the pantry. A full list for 78 kinds of produce.
  • Can you freeze it? A freezing chart for fruits and vegetablesWhich fruits and vegetables freeze well, which need blanching first, and which don't freeze well at all, like lettuce and cucumbers. Blanching times included.
  • How to organize your fridge so produce lasts longerKeep the fridge at 40°F or below, put things that wilt in the high-humidity drawer and things that rot in the low-humidity drawer, and keep apples away from greens.
