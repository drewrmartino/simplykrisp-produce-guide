How to store mangoes

Mangoes keep best on the counter until ripe, then in the fridge, at about 56°F, and last 5-7 days ripe. Here is how to store them, what to keep them away from, and how to pick good mangoes.

Where to keep them
Counter, then fridge once ripe
How long they last
5-7 days ripe (refrigerated)
Best temperature
About 56°F (13°C)
Don't store next to
Apples and bananas

How to store mangoes

  • Ripen on the counter at room temperature
  • Once ripe, refrigerate to extend freshness by several days
  • To speed ripening, place in a paper bag
  • Store cut mango in an airtight container in the fridge

Picking good mangoes: A ripe mango gives slightly when squeezed and has a fruity aroma at the stem end. Color is not a reliable indicator.

Does a Krisp help? Mangoes ripen on the counter, and once ripe they have only a day or two. A Krisp adds little.

Mangoes and ethylene

Ethylene is a gas that ripening produce gives off. In a closed crisper drawer it builds up and makes sensitive produce yellow, soften or sprout sooner.

  • Mangoes give off a moderate amount of ethylene as they ripen, enough to speed up sensitive produce in a closed drawer.
  • Mangoes are somewhat sensitive to ethylene, so storing them away from ripening fruit helps.

Keep them apart from apples and bananas.

The part people throw away

About 29% of mangoes by weight is usually trimmed off as pit and skin (USDA FoodData Central). Some of it can still be used:

  • Scrape the fruit left on the pit into a smoothie
  • Mango skin can irritate people sensitive to poison ivy, so compost it
