How to store cauliflower, and why it goes bad

Cauliflower shows every problem on its white head. It discolors around ethylene, browns where it was bumped, and grows dark spots as it ages.

Where to keep it
Fridge
How long it lasts
1-2 weeks (refrigerated)
Best temperature
About 32°F (0°C)
Don't store next to
Apples, pears and tomatoes
What are you seeing?
Discolored head and yellow leavesBrown, bruised patchesDark spots, fuzz or slime

How to store cauliflower

  • Store stem-side up in a loose bag in the crisper
  • Do not wash until ready to cook
  • Wrap in a damp paper towel inside a bag
  • Brown spots can be trimmed — the rest is still good

Picking good cauliflower: Compact, creamy white heads with no dark spots. Leaves should be green and fresh.

Why cauliflower goes bad

Discolored head and yellow leaves

Ethylene gasTrim and use

What you'll seeThe white head turns dull or yellowish-brown, and the green leaves yellow and drop off.

Why it happens

UC Davis notes that even low levels of ethylene discolor the head and make the outer leaves yellow and detach. 1

Is it still OK?

Trim any discolored surface and pull off the yellow leaves. The rest is fine if it's firm and smells fresh.

Prevent it next time
  • Keep it away from apples, pears and tomatoes
  • Store it in a loose bag in the crisper drawer
Use it up
  • Roast it
  • Rice it or blend it into soup
Where a Krisp fits

A Krisp makes a real difference here. Slows browning on the head and yellowing leaves. Expect about 2–3 extra days in a closed crisper drawer. See the Double Krisp

Brown, bruised patches

BruisingTrim and use

What you'll seeBrown patches where the head was pressed or bumped.

Why it happens

Bruised cauliflower browns quickly and decays sooner. 12

Is it still OK?

Shave or cut off the brown patches. The rest is still good.

Prevent it next time
  • Store it stem side up, so the head isn't resting on the shelf
  • Don't stack anything on it
Use it up
  • Roast or steam the rest

Dark spots, fuzz or slime

MoldTrim and use

What you'll seeSmall dark spots on the head that spread, sometimes gray fuzz, or soft, slimy, bad-smelling patches.

Why it happens

Black spot and gray mold are fungal. Bacterial soft rot turns the head soft and slimy. All of them get more likely after three to four weeks of storage, or sooner if the cauliflower is kept too warm. 13

Is it still OK?

Cauliflower is firm, so USDA's rule for firm vegetables applies: cut off small mold spots with at least an inch around and below them. If the spots are widespread, or the head is soft, slimy or smells bad, toss it.

Prevent it next time
  • Don't wash it until you cook it
  • Use it within one to two weeks
Use it up
  • Cook the trimmed head the same day

What went wrong?

Cauliflower and ethylene

Ethylene is a gas that ripening produce gives off. In a closed crisper drawer it builds up and makes sensitive produce yellow, soften or sprout sooner.

  • Cauliflower gives off almost no ethylene, so it is safe to store next to most other produce.
  • Cauliflower is very sensitive to ethylene. Even a little in a closed drawer speeds up how fast it ages.

Keep it apart from apples, pears and tomatoes.

The part people throw away

About 61% of cauliflower by weight is usually trimmed off as leaves and core (USDA FoodData Central). Some of it can still be used:

  • Roast the leaves with oil and salt, they crisp like chips
  • Dice the core into soup or rice it with the florets
